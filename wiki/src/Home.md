# OpenGonio wiki

Open-source four-axis gonioreflectometer and 3D scanner. It measures the bidirectional reflectance distribution function (BRDF) of flat samples and scans objects in 3D. Design follows @R1, rebuilt for under $630 in parts, with no soldering, drilling, sawing or crimping.

New here? The **project map** on the website (`docs/explore/`) shows the whole project as a clickable map with a guided tour in plain words.
Inside (`docs/inside.html`) opens up the software, tests, firmware, wiring and roadmap, and the control app (`docs/app/`) runs in the browser on the simulated machine.
