Machine wiring, step by step

The rest of the electronics on the bench, after the power and controller walkthroughs: table, tilt and spin motors, their endstops, the lamp, the camera, then the optional fan and spectral sensor. Dotted wires were made on the earlier pages; the moving wire with the two rings is the one to make now. Each check (✓) tests that group before the next, and the last one runs the whole machine. Wording from docs/wiring.yaml, the same source as the full wiring guide.

Press the e-stop and keep the wall adapter unplugged while you wire. Plug the adapter in only where a check says live. Never plug or unplug a motor with motor power on. Never light the lamp off its heatsink.

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design. Part numbers in brackets are the parts list IDs (README → Electronics). These are sections 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 of the full wiring guide; section 9 (standalone station) comes with stage 15 and section 10 (colour LED bank) is blocked.
