The build book

Every printed and bought part, in the order it goes on. Parts already on the machine are grey; each step's new parts are in colour and slide into place along the arrows. Drag the picture to turn it; double-click to put it back.

Print this (in black ABS) Buy thisAlready on Whole book on one page, to print or save as PDF

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

The model is the CAD assembly (tools/cad/assembly.py); the words are the stage checklists (.github/phases.yml); the order is docs/assembly.yaml. Wires, endstops, flags, the rim and tilt belts, spacers and tape are not modelled: the checklist line says where they go, and every wire is in the wiring guide. Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design.
