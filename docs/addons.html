Hub add-ons, step by step

Two optional parts on the ESP32-S3 hub: the AS7341 spectral sensor, and a 2 in status screen that shows the machine's state on the machine itself (motor power, keys, supply, laptop link, run lines), so you can still see what is going on if the laptop app has a problem. Two lever connectors share the hub's 3V3 and GND. Wording from docs/wiring.yaml, the same source as the full wiring guide (section 8).

Unplug the hub's USB while you wire it. Nothing here touches the 24 V side.

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design. Status screen: Waveshare 2inch LCD Module (STA-09, price [TBD]) on the printed stand and bezel (STA-10: four M2 × 12 into the deck pilots beside the controller tray, four M2 × 8 through the bezel). It uses the hub pins of encoders 2 and 3; SCREEN OFF gives them back.
