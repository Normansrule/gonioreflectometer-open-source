Wiring, one wire at a time

Every connection in the machine, in the order you make it. The pulsing tungsten rings mark the two ends of the wire to make now; the moving dots run from where it starts to where it lands; a dashed outline shows the wire after it. Wires you have already made stay drawn.

Press the e-stop and unplug the wall adapter before touching any terminal. Strip 8 mm for screw terminals and 11 mm for lever connectors, twist the strands, tug-test every wire.

First time? The power wiring walkthrough draws the first wires as the parts sit on the bench, the controller walkthrough carries on to the keypads and power sense, and the machine walkthrough wires and tests the other motors, endstops, lamp and camera.

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design. Part numbers in brackets are the parts list IDs (README → Electronics). Printed parts and how they go together: the build book.
