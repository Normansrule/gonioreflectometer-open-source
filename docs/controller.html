Controller wiring, step by step

Stage 02 on the bench, after the power wiring: both boards to the laptop, the bench motor and its endstop, the two keypads and the power sense. Wires already made stay drawn; the moving wire with the two rings is the one to make now. The checks (✓) carry the flashing and test commands, so the board is proven before the next group of wires. Wording from docs/wiring.yaml, the same source as the full wiring guide.

Press the e-stop and keep the wall adapter unplugged while you wire. Plug the adapter in only where a check says live. Never plug or unplug a motor with motor power on.

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design. Part numbers in brackets are the parts list IDs (README → Electronics). The other motors, their endstops and the lamp are wired with their stages: full wiring guide, sections 3–5.
