Power wiring, step by step

The motor power drawn the way the parts sit on the bench: wall adapter and jack, e-stop, over-travel switch, the two lever connectors on the tray, the SKR Pico and the PC817 power sense. Wires already made stay drawn; the moving wire with the two rings is the one to make now. The wording comes from docs/wiring.yaml, the same source as the full wiring guide, which carries on from here with the motors.

Press the e-stop and keep the wall adapter unplugged while you wire. Strip 8 mm for screw terminals and 11 mm for lever connectors, twist the strands, tug-test every wire.

Keys: ← →. Each step has its own link (the address bar).

Nothing here has been built or measured yet: this is the design. Part numbers in brackets are the parts list IDs (README → Electronics). The wires here are sections 1 and 2 of the full wiring guide plus the two power-sense wires from its section 6; after the check, Next carries on with the controller wiring.
